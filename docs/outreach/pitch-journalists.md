# Pitch templates — sports business journalists

Target list (US sports betting vertical):
- Legal Sports Report (legalsportsreport.com)
- Covers (covers.com/news)
- Sports Handle (sportshandle.com)
- SBC Americas (sbcamericas.com)
- Play USA (playusa.com)
- iGaming Business (igamingbusiness.com)
- Gambling.com (gambling.com/news)
- Local beat writers on the sports-betting beat (AP, Reuters, Bloomberg,
  NYT DealBook, WSJ)

International gambling trade press:
- EGR (egr.global)
- Gambling Insider (gamblinginsider.com)
- CalvinAyre (calvinayre.com)

## General rules

1. **Subject line under 60 characters.** Journalists delete anything longer
   in the preview pane.
2. **First sentence answers: why am I getting this?** Don't bury the lede.
3. **Offer a data hook, not a product.** Journalists write about stories,
   not apps. See `data-journalism-briefs.md`.
4. **Attach nothing.** Put media in the press kit at `/press` and link it.
5. **One ask per email.** Either "cover this feature" OR "use this data"
   OR "include in roundup" — never three asks in one email.
6. **Follow up once, 5-7 business days later.** Never more than twice.
