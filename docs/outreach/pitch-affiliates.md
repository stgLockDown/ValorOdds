# Pitch templates — affiliate sites & content creators

## Target list (search-driven)

Run these queries and compile the top 30 results from each:

- `site:*.com "arbitrage betting" (tool OR app OR platform)`
- `"best odds comparison" (review OR best of)`
- `"positive ev" betting (tool OR software)`
- `site:reddit.com/r/sportsbook "valor odds" OR "rundown" OR "oddsjam"`
- `best sportsbook tools [year]`

Prioritize sites that:
- Rank top 20 for any of the above queries
- Publish regularly (last post within 60 days)
- Already link to at least one competitor (OddsJam, ArbPicker, Unabated, etc.)

## Measuring affiliate outreach

Track in a simple spreadsheet:

| Site | Contact | Date sent | Follow-up 1 | Follow-up 2 | Status | Link placed? | Affiliate sign-up? |

Status enum:
- `open` — pitch sent, no reply yet
- `interested` — they replied, conversation ongoing
- `placed` — link went live
- `affiliate` — they joined the program
- `declined` — they said no
- `cold` — no response after two follow-ups

Hit rate benchmark:
- 10-15% reply rate on cold email
- 30-40% conversion from reply → some kind of yes
- So target: ~4% of cold pitches end in a placed link.

A realistic first quarter: 200 pitches → ~8-12 placed links → 2-4
affiliates signed.
