# Pitch templates — sports betting podcasts

## Target list

**Tier 1 — established podcasts with >10k weekly downloads:**
- Bet The Process (Rufus Peabody)
- The Action Network podcasts
- Circles Off (Rob Pizzola, Johnny from Harvard)
- Sharp Angles
- The Favorites (Ringer)
- Against All Odds (Cousin Sal / Bill Simmons)
- VSiN podcasts
- Steve Fezzik's show
- The Lion's Edge

**Tier 2 — growing niche podcasts, higher conversion per listener:**
- Arbitrage-specific and +EV-specific YouTube channels
- Subreddit-adjacent creators (r/sportsbook regulars)
- DFS / prop-focused podcasts (Establish The Run, PrizePicks-adjacent)

## Two plays

1. **Sponsorship (paid):** Fastest growth lever. Budget: $50-500 per mid-roll
   on tier 2, $1k-5k on tier 1. Track via unique promo code + landing page.
2. **Guest appearance (earned):** Higher credibility, slower. Pitch the
   founder or head of data as a guest on arbitrage / line-movement /
   data-driven betting episodes.

## Measurement

Every sponsorship gets:

1. **Unique promo code** (e.g. `PODCASTNAME`) for 20% off first month.
2. **Unique landing page** at `valorodds.com/r/<podcastname>` with tracked
   UTM.
3. **30-day cookie window** for attribution.
4. **Weekly email report** to the host showing clicks, signups, and
   payout earned if they're on the affiliate program.
